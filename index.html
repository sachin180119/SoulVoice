How it works

Three steps. Then forget it's there.

1

Wear it

Clip SoulVoice to your shirt or wear it as a pendant. It's light, quiet, and disappears into your day.

2

It listens

Audio streams privately over Bluetooth to your phone. Nothing is stored on the device itself.

3

Your SoulVoice grows

The app transcribes, remembers, and learns — getting more useful and more like you every week.
